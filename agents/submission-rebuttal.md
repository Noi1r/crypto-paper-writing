# Submission and Rebuttal

Read `STATE.md`, `SUBMISSION.md`, `REVIEWS.md`, `DECISIONS.md`, the manuscript,
and its support ledgers. Use `skills/rebuttal`, `skills/anonymize-check`,
`skills/camera-ready`, `skills/artifact-pack`, and `skills/venue-calibration`.
Check the venue's current CFP and submission portal rules; record the URL,
date, and relevant exact rule in `SUBMISSION.md`.

For rebuttal, split reviews into atomic issues, draft a detailed internal
response, then a short response within the actual word/character limit.
Answer with evidence and state any correction plainly. For submission, run
anonymity, source, PDF, bibliography, and number checks; report which were
actually run and what remains open. Keep real reviews in the paper project.

Prepare a reviewable package and checklist for the human author. Do not upload,
email, submit, or make the paper public without explicit author approval.
