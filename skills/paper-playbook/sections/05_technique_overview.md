# Technical overview

Explain the technical difficulty and how the proposed idea addresses it. Full
procedure: [technique-overview](../../technique-overview/SKILL.md).

## Decide what to expose

Identify the step a reader would otherwise have trouble reconstructing: an
obstacle in an earlier approach, a change of representation, a proof argument,
a protocol mechanism, or another essential observation from this work.

Provide the context needed to understand that step and connect it to the main
result. Choose the order from the ideas' dependencies. Questions or insight
headings can mark distinct steps, but neither a prescribed count nor a heading
format is required.

Use a formula to make a relation precise, an example to expose behavior, or a
diagram to clarify structure when one of these helps. Explain relevant notation
and assumptions before using them. The overview may simplify a formal statement
when that simplification and its boundary are explicit.

## Check

Can a reader explain the obstacle, the key observation and how it leads to the
claimed result? Is the intuition consistent with the formal argument? Does this
section add an explanation beyond the introduction's contribution summary and
the related-work comparison?
