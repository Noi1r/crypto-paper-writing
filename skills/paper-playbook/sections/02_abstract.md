# Abstract

The abstract should let a reader identify the specific problem, the key method
and the main established results. Full procedure:
[abstract-craft](../../abstract-craft/SKILL.md).

## Decisions

Use enough context for the result to be intelligible. A familiar problem may need
very little background; an unfamiliar model may need a short explanation first.
Explain the technical idea at a useful level, then state the result with the
conditions needed to interpret it. A theorem or construction can be the result;
an experimental number or artifact sentence is not mandatory.

Use one or several paragraphs according to the content and venue requirements.
Select results for their importance to the paper, without giving every
contribution an equal sentence allocation. Follow confirmed author preferences
when they fit this abstract.

## Check

Is the problem specific? Does the method explain what the paper does? Are the
claimed results supported in the body with the same assumptions and scope? Can
the abstract be understood without definitions available only later in the paper?
