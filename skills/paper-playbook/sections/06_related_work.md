# Related work

Explain the relationships that matter for understanding the contribution. Full
procedure: [related-work-writing](../../related-work-writing/SKILL.md).

## Organize for the comparison

Choose groups that reflect the actual literature and the reader's needs: shared
questions, techniques, assumptions, guarantees, costs or application settings.
A historical sequence can help when the development of a question matters.

For the closest work, give enough detail to compare the specific results. State
when settings or assumptions differ. Use a table when its rows share meaningful
comparison dimensions; explain qualifications in the table or nearby text.

Place the section early if it establishes the problem context, or later if the
comparison needs details introduced by the paper. Avoid repeating a full
comparison in the introduction, overview and related-work section.

## Check

Are the central characterizations supported by primary sources? Is the paper's
specific advance clear? Are complementary and differently scoped results
represented fairly? Have the relevant citations and compared versions been
verified?
