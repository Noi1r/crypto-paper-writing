# Title

Identify the paper through its actual problem, object, method or main result.
Use the abstract and main statements to decide what should be prominent.

## Choices

- A method-led title helps when the mechanism is the main contribution.
- A result-led title helps when a bound, capability or achieved property is the
  central message. Keep any qualifier needed to make that message true.
- A problem-led title helps when a recognizable question connects several results.

Use terminology the intended readership can understand. Avoid a vague claim of
quality where a concrete object or result would be more informative. Check that
scope in the title agrees with the paper and that the title fits the venue's
format without changing its typography.

When alternatives would help the author decide, offer a small set with genuinely
different emphases and explain their tradeoffs. Preserve a clear existing title
unless a change is requested or a scope mismatch needs correction.
