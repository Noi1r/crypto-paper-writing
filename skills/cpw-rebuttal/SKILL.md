# Prepare an author response

Read the review file, manuscript, `STATE.md`, `REVIEWS.md`, `DECISIONS.md` and
relevant support records. Obtain the actual response limit and applicable venue
rules from the supplied material or current official source; ask if a necessary
constraint remains unknown.

Follow `.paper-writing/skills/rebuttal/SKILL.md`. Split reviews into specific
issues, draft a detailed response, and then prepare the submission-length text.
Answer the decisive questions with support, state corrections plainly, and avoid
promising an unperformed experiment or an unproved result.

Keep real reviews and response drafts in the paper project. Report unresolved
issues and the measured word or character count. Preparing a response does not
authorize sending it; the author approves any external submission.
