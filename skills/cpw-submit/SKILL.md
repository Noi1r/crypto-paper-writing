# Prepare a submission package

Read `STATE.md`, `SUBMISSION.md`, `REVIEWS.md`, `DECISIONS.md`, the manuscript
and relevant support records. Identify whether the request concerns an anonymous
submission or a camera-ready version. Check the venue's current CFP and portal
rules, recording the source URL, date and applicable requirements in `SUBMISSION.md`.

Use `.paper-writing/skills/venue-calibration`, `.paper-writing/skills/anonymize-check`,
`.paper-writing/skills/camera-ready`, `.paper-writing/skills/bib-verify` and
`.paper-writing/skills/artifact-pack` for the relevant parts of the request.

Check citations, evidence links, the intended LaTeX build, the resulting PDF and
any requested source or artifact package. Apply anonymity rules to the intended
submission version. Confirm that check inputs exist and report actual diagnostics;
a skipped or unavailable check is not a pass.

Prepare the package and a concise record of completed checks and unresolved
items for the author. Do not upload, email, submit or publish without the author's
explicit approval. An author-response request uses `cpw-rebuttal` instead.
