# Create manuscript figures and tables

Read `STATE.md`, the intended claim in `CLAIMS.md`, the relevant rows in
`EVIDENCE.md`, and the manuscript location. Use `.paper-writing/skills/paper-figures`,
`.paper-writing/figures/PATTERNS.md`, and the synthetic templates in `.paper-writing/figures/templates/`.
`.paper-writing/skills/figure-harvest` may be used on a local public-PDF corpus for design
inspiration; never copy a harvested figure into the repository or paper.

Generate a PDF figure from a script and the paper project's actual data. Keep
the data path, axes, units, legend, and caption explicit. Check at print size,
in grayscale, and against the text's numerical scope. Put the generated source
and figure under the paper project and identify its intended manuscript location.
