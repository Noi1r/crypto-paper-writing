# Extraction provenance

This toolkit was extracted on 2026-09-29 from the public
[`crypto-research-agents`](https://github.com/acprk/crypto-research-agents)
repository, starting at commit `bfe2303`.

The writing skills, LNCS/rebuttal/handoff templates, figure kit, public reading
lists, and evidence-ledger parser retain their original MIT attribution.
The role prompts, `/cpw-*` commands, installer, and top-level guidance were
adapted to operate without the research-phase orchestrator or `crbench` runner.
Examples in this repository are synthetic. No paper-specific research content
was intentionally copied.
